## Eligibility and rules requiring owner review
Legal-majority individuals, teams up to 3, and eligible organizations may enter. Residency exclusions include Quebec, Argentina, Brazil, Italy, Philippines, Russia, Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria, Belarus, Crimea, DNR, LNR and UAE; sanctions/local-law restrictions also apply. Organizer/judge affiliations and conflicts can exclude entrants. Confirm personal facts privately; do not infer them from account access.

Only new work created during the submission period qualifies. Standard libraries/templates are permitted; disclose other pre-existing work. Genuine Kiro use is mandatory. Third-party rights and licenses must be respected. Working judge access must remain free through judging. English materials or translations are required. Submission creates contractual obligations; owner review is required before entering. Judging weights impact, implementation, design, innovation and presentation equally. [Official rules](https://kiroween.devpost.com/rules)

## Rule text confirmed 2026-11-16 (owner-pasted from the official rules page)
These points are stricter or more explicit than the summary above:
- "Insufficient use of Kiro will result in disqualification at the Judges' sole discretion." This is a subjective judge call, so authentic `.kiro` specs/hooks/steering and a real build record matter more than a checkbox.
- The project must install and run consistently on its stated platform and function as depicted in the video and text. The demo must not show anything the repository cannot do.
- Code must be on **GitHub** (not GitLab or Bitbucket), public, with an open-source license file that GitHub detects and shows in the About section.
- The `/.kiro` directory must be at the repository root. **Do not add `.kiro` or any of its sub-folders to `.gitignore`**; the rules say this could disqualify the entry.
- Skeleton Crew: one repository containing **two separate folders** for the two applications.
- The entry must name a main category **and** a bonus category.
- Third-party SDKs, APIs and data must be used under their terms. This applies to model providers and to any 1-SEC material.

## Best category fit and submission checklist
**Skeleton Crew** is the closest fit: a shared template demonstrated through two distinct applications. The organizer asks for a public GitHub repository with source/assets/run instructions and an open-source license, root `.kiro` evidence, and two separate application folders. Also provide working app access, a feature description, a public YouTube demonstration under 3 minutes, and an explanation of genuine Kiro use. The alternative categories are Resurrection, Frankenstein and Costume Contest; spooky naming alone is not a strong technical differentiation.
